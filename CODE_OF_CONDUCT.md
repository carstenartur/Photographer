# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and technically constructive community. Participation must be free from harassment and discrimination, regardless of experience, identity, background, disability, appearance, nationality, religion, age or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behaviour

Participants should:

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- give and receive technical feedback respectfully;
- distinguish verified behaviour from hypotheses and unsupported claims;
- protect credentials, personal data, camera identifiers and private source material;
- respect the CR3/CRX clean-room boundary and third-party licences;
- accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety and repository policy.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, threats, insults, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, publication of credentials, sustained disruption and knowingly submitting prohibited proprietary material.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, releases and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the maintainer at [carsten.hammer@t-online.de](mailto:carsten.hammer@t-online.de). Security vulnerabilities must instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation or permanently exclude a participant when necessary. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, subject to legal and safety obligations.
